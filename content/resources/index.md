These guides are for the people who run buildings: office managers, property and facility managers, strata committees and practice owners. Each one answers a question we are asked on walkthroughs, in plain language, with sources where a claim needs one.

We organise them by topic rather than by date. Start with the topic closest to your problem and follow the links down into the detail, or jump straight to the guide you need.

## How the guides are organised

Every topic has an overview page that covers the whole subject, then deeper guides on each part of it. If a guide mentions a term or a decision that deserves more space, it links to the page that covers it properly.

## Who writes these

The guides are written and reviewed by the team that runs Vantage Point Facility Services, from what we see cleaning commercial buildings on the Gold Coast and in Brisbane. Where a rule, standard or statistic matters, we cite the regulator or standards body so you can check it yourself. If you spot something out of date, tell us and we will fix it.

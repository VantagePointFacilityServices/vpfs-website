An eco-friendly cleaning policy only matters if it ends up in the contract. Buyers who simply ask for "green cleaning" get whatever the contractor means by it. Buyers who write specific, checkable requirements into the scope get something they can inspect and enforce. This cluster shows how.

General information for buyers. Check ecolabel and regulator pages for the current position before you rely on them.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Writing eco requirements into a cleaning scope** gives you wording and a checklist.
- **Eco cleaning for strata and body corporates** covers the extra considerations when a committee is the buyer.
- **Does eco cleaning cost more?** explains what moves the price and how to compare fairly.

They follow on from [understanding eco claims](/resources/eco-friendly-cleaning/understanding-eco-claims/) and sit under the main [eco-friendly commercial cleaning buyer's guide](/resources/eco-friendly-cleaning/). To discuss your requirements, [book a walkthrough](/contact.html).

Greener cleaning is not only about which bottle is used. It is about the product, the way it is diluted and applied, and the equipment that does the work. A certified product used badly can be wasteful, and a conventional product used precisely can have a smaller footprint than a poorly used green one. Understanding both sides helps you ask better questions and write better requirements.

General information for buyers. Check ecolabel and regulator pages for the current position before you rely on them.

Eco claims are everywhere in cleaning tenders, and they are hard to compare because they come in so many forms: a logo, a product name, a sentence in a proposal or a single line in a sales call. Some are backed by an independent standard and some are not. This cluster helps you tell the difference, quickly.

General information for buyers. Check ecolabel and regulator pages for the current position before you rely on them.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Ecolabels and certifications explained** covers the Australian and overseas programs you are likely to meet, what each certifies and how to check one.
- **Greenwashing red flags in cleaning tenders** lists the warning signs and the questions that expose them.

Both sit under the main [eco-friendly commercial cleaning buyer's guide](/resources/eco-friendly-cleaning/), and both feed the practical checklist in [how to verify a contractor's eco claims](/resources/eco-friendly-cleaning/how-to-verify-eco-cleaning-claims/). If you are running a formal process, use them with [how to run a cleaning tender](/resources/hiring-a-cleaner/tenders-and-rfqs/how-to-run-a-cleaning-tender/).

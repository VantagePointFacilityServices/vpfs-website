Industrial and warehouse sites mix several kinds of space under one roof: offices at the front, open floor and racking in the middle, and amenities and lunch rooms for the workforce. Each needs a different approach, and the safety rules of the site apply to cleaners as much as to anyone else.

A written scope matters even more here than in an office, because the site's hazards, shift patterns and operational spill rules decide what a cleaner can safely do. Without them, contractors price different jobs and the cleaner ends up deciding what is safe on their own.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Warehouse and factory cleaning scope** explains how to scope the floor, dust, offices and risk controls.
- **Amenities and lunch rooms on industrial sites** covers the spaces your workers use every day and the obligation to keep them clean.

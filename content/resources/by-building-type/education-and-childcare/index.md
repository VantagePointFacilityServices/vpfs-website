Schools and childcare centres are cleaned around children, so the usual office assumptions do not hold. The hours are fixed by the timetable, the surfaces are touched constantly, the people in the building are the most vulnerable to illness and the standard for safety and screening is higher.

Getting this right protects more than cleanliness. A site where hygiene is visibly looked after earns the trust of parents, staff and regulators, and a contractor who understands the setting will raise hazards, damage and health concerns early instead of working around them.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Cleaning schools and early-learning centres** covers the scope, hours, safety and screening for a site with children.
- **Term-break deep cleans** shows how to plan the periodic cleaning that cannot happen during term.

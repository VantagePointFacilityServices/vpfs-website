A term-break deep clean is a scheduled, more intensive clean done while children are away. Plan it early, scope the tasks that cannot be done during term, confirm access and timing with the contractor, and check the result before reopening day. Treat it as a project with a start, a finish and a sign-off.

## Why do it in the break?

Some work needs empty rooms and time: carpet extraction, hard-floor restoration, moving furniture, cleaning behind and under equipment, and detailed cleaning of fixtures and fittings. The break is the only time these can be done without disruption, and it resets the standard for the next term.

## What should the scope include?

Typical items to specify:

- Carpet cleaning or extraction in classrooms and play areas.
- Hard floors scrubbed and, where appropriate, recoated.
- Furniture, shelves and equipment cleaned, including undersides and legs.
- Washrooms and nappy-change areas cleaned in depth, including grout, vents and fixtures.
- Kitchens and food-preparation areas cleaned in depth.
- Internal windows and glass, light fittings and high dusting.
- Walls and doors spot-cleaned where marks are cleaning rather than painting.

Say what is excluded, such as external windows at height or specialist equipment, and who moves furniture back.

## How should you plan the timing?

Agree dates as early as you can, since contractors' calendars fill around school holidays. Allow time for floors and carpets to dry, and for anything that needs a second visit. Leave buffer between the end of the clean and the reopening day.

## What access arrangements are needed?

Confirm keys, alarms and any contractor induction before the break. Agree who is on site, when and how the contractor reaches a contact. Make sure other trades, such as maintenance and painting, are scheduled so they do not undo the clean.

## How do you check the result?

Walk the site with a checklist that mirrors the scope before the contractor leaves, and note anything to fix. Take dated photos. Ask for the contractor to return and correct issues at no charge.

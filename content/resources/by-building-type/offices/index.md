Office cleaning looks simple, but most problems come from three decisions made loosely: what the scope covers and how often, how cleaners get in and out of the building after hours, and what happens when a tenancy ends. These guides cover each one.

Offices are the most common building type we clean, and the one where a clear scope pays off fastest, because the work is repeated on a schedule and small gaps compound over weeks.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Office cleaning scope and frequency** shows how to set tasks and frequencies area by area.
- **After-hours access and security** covers keys, alarms, inductions and who is responsible.
- **Tenancy handover and end-of-lease cleaning** explains how to prepare a commercial tenancy for handback.

Read them alongside the [guide to commercial cleaning by building type](/resources/by-building-type/) and the [buyer's guide to hiring a cleaner](/resources/hiring-a-cleaner/). To have your office scoped, [book a walkthrough](/contact.html).

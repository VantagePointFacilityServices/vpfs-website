A strata or body corporate cleaning contract is bought on behalf of owners, usually by a committee of volunteers. That changes what a good guide looks like: the scope has to be easy for non-experts to read, the decision has to be defensible to owners and the reporting has to show committee members that they are getting what they pay for.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Common-area cleaning scope** covers what to include for lobbies, lifts, stairwells, car parks and amenities.
- **How committees evaluate cleaning contractors** sets out a fair process, from quotes to approval.
- **Reporting to a strata committee** shows what information a committee needs and how often.

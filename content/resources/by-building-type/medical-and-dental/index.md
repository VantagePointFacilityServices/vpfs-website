Cleaning a medical or dental practice is different from cleaning an office, because the building is part of how you manage infection risk. That does not mean the cleaner is responsible for your clinical protocols. It means you need a contractor who understands the difference between clinical and non-clinical areas, follows your written procedures and can show that they did.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Infection-control expectations in clinics** explains who is responsible for what and what to require of a contractor.
- **Clinical and non-clinical zones** shows how to divide the practice into zones and specify cleaning for each.

These guides are general information for buyers, not clinical advice. Your practice's infection-control requirements come from the standards and guidelines that apply to your profession, and your own infection-control plan comes first.

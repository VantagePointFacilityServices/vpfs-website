A cleaning contract and a quality process are two halves of one protection. The contract says what you are entitled to and what happens when it is not delivered. The quality process is how you find out whether it was. A strong contract with no inspection routine goes unenforced, and a good inspection routine with a weak contract leaves you with no remedy.

These guides cover both. They are written for the person who signs and manages the contract: a facility or property manager, an office manager, or a strata committee representative.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Cleaning contract terms to check** lists the clauses that matter most before you sign: scope, term, price changes, variations, performance standards and exit.
- **How cleaning quality is audited** explains what an inspection actually involves, who should carry it out and how often.
- **What a monthly cleaning report should show** describes a report you can act on, not a page of reassurance.

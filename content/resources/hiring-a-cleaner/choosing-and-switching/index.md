Choosing a cleaner and changing one are two sides of the same decision. Most people only think hard about it when something has gone wrong with the current contractor, which is the worst time to improve it. These guides cover the questions that separate a reliable contractor from a risky one, how to leave a cleaner without leaving your building in a mess, and the early signs that a contract is slipping.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Questions to ask a commercial cleaner** gives you the questions to put to any contractor before you hire, and what a good answer sounds like.
- **How to change cleaning contractor without disruption** walks through notice, handover, access and the first weeks with a new team.
- **Warning signs your cleaner is underperforming** shows what to look for, how to document it and when to act.

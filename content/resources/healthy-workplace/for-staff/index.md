For most businesses the case for cleaning starts with the people who work in the building. Staff share washrooms, kitchens, door handles and meeting rooms every day, and they notice immediately when those spaces are neglected. The questions managers ask are practical: does cleaning really reduce sickness, which spaces matter most and how often should they be cleaned?

These guides answer those questions honestly. They separate what health authorities say from the confident-sounding statistics that circulate in supplier marketing, and they turn the sensible parts into specific frequencies you can put in a scope.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Does a clean office reduce sick days?** looks at the evidence and the limits of it.
- **Shared kitchens and break rooms** covers the space where food, hands and shared equipment meet.
- **High-touch surfaces: what to clean and how often** gives a working list you can adapt.

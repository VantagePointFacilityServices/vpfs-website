In a shared workplace kitchen, the cleaner should clean benches, sinks, appliance exteriors, floors and bins on a set schedule, while staff look after their own food, dishes and the inside of the fridge. Agreeing that split in writing is what keeps break rooms clean between visits and stops arguments about who should have cleaned what.

## Why do break rooms need special attention?

Break rooms mix food, hands and shared equipment in one small space, and everyone uses them. A dirty kitchen is the most common complaint in office buildings, and it affects how people feel about their workplace more than almost any other room.

## What should the cleaner do?

| Task | Typical frequency |
|---|---|
| Wipe benches, tables and sinks | Every visit |
| Clean and sanitise tap handles, kettle and microwave handles | Every visit |
| Wipe appliance exteriors, including fridge and dishwasher doors | Weekly |
| Empty bins and replace liners | Every visit |
| Clean floor | Every visit |
| Clean inside microwave | Weekly, or as agreed |
| Restock consumables such as soap, paper towel and detergent | Every visit |

These are examples to adapt, not a standard. Busier kitchens need more.

## What should staff do?

Staff normally clear their own dishes and food, wipe up spills as they happen and clear out the fridge on an agreed day. Many offices set a weekly fridge clear-out, with a note to staff that anything left is thrown away. Cleaners do not usually clean inside fridges, handle personal food or wash individual dishes unless the scope says so.

## How do you keep it working?

Put a short notice in the room that says what staff do and what cleaners do. Provide the basics: bins, dish rack, detergent and a cleaning cloth. Review it if complaints return. Our guide to [office cleaning scope and frequency](/resources/by-building-type/offices/office-cleaning-scope-and-frequency/) shows how to set these out for the whole building.

## What about hygiene guidance?

Health authorities recommend regular hand washing and cleaning of frequently touched surfaces. See [does a clean office reduce sick days?](/resources/healthy-workplace/for-staff/does-a-clean-office-reduce-sick-days/). In a break room that means taps, handles, microwave and fridge doors, and a hand-washing option with soap.

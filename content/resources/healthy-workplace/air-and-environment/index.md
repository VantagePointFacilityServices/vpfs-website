Indoor air quality is often the first thing people mention when they feel an office is stuffy or unhealthy, and cleaning is often blamed or credited for it. The truth is more mixed. Cleaning controls the dust and dirt that settle in a building, and the products and methods it uses can themselves affect the air. But ventilation, building materials and the way a building is used matter just as much, and cleaning alone will not fix a ventilation problem.

These guides help you see where cleaning contributes, where it does not, and how to adjust during the seasons when illness is most common.

Visitors notice the entry, the reception area, the lift and the washroom first, so these should be cleaned to a higher and more consistent standard than the rest of the building. Specify them separately in the scope, with specific tasks and check them yourself as a visitor would.

## What do visitors notice?

They see the entry glass and doors, the floor at the door, the reception counter and seating, and the first thing that smells or looks wrong. In multi-tenant buildings they also see the lobby, lifts and corridors, which may not be in your control. They use the washroom and often remember it, good or bad.

## What should the scope say about visitor areas?

| Area | Tasks to specify |
|---|---|
| Entry and glass | Clean glass doors and frames, wipe handles, sweep or vacuum entry mat and threshold |
| Reception | Wipe counter and seating, dust ledges, remove fingerprints, tidy magazines if provided, empty bins |
| Meeting rooms used by visitors | Wipe tables, check chairs, vacuum, empty bins, reset |
| Lifts and lobbies | Wipe buttons and walls, clean floors |
| Visitor washrooms | Full clean, restock, check for odours, mirrors streak-free |

## Why does timing matter?

Cleaning that finishes after midnight can be undone by morning: a wet entry floor, a dropped coffee, an overflowing bin. Agree how the cleaner leaves the space ready for the day and whether a mid-day or morning check is needed for busy sites.

## How do you check it?

Walk in through the front door as a visitor would, at the time they would arrive, and look and smell. Photograph issues and raise them against the scope. Our guide to [how cleaning quality is audited](/resources/hiring-a-cleaner/contracts-and-quality/how-cleaning-quality-is-audited/) explains a routine.

## What about the building you do not control?

If lobbies and lifts belong to the building owner or the body corporate, raise issues with them, and make sure your own tenancy entry is clean. In strata buildings, see [common-area cleaning scope](/resources/by-building-type/strata-and-body-corporate/common-area-cleaning-scope/).

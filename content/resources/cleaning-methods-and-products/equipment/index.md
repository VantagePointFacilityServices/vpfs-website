Equipment is where a contractor's investment shows. A cleaner with the right machines and cloths does a better job in less time, and one making do with poor equipment either takes longer, which you pay for, or cuts corners, which you see. You do not need to be an expert, but a few basics let you ask sensible questions.

This is written for the person buying or managing cleaning, so you can understand and check what a contractor is doing. It is not a training manual for cleaners.

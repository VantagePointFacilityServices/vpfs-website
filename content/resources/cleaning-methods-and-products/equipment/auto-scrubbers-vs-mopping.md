An auto-scrubber is the better choice for large, open hard floors because it applies solution, scrubs and picks up the dirty water in one pass, covering far more floor in less time than mopping. Mopping suits small, cluttered or awkward spaces. Many sites use both, and the right mix depends on layout.

This is written for the person buying or managing cleaning, so you can understand and check what a contractor is doing. It is not a training manual for cleaners.

## What does each do well?

| | Auto-scrubber | Mopping |
|---|---|---|
| Large open floors | Strong: fast and consistent | Slow and uneven over large areas |
| Small or cluttered rooms | Awkward, hard to manoeuvre | Practical |
| Edges and corners | Needs hand-finishing | Easier to reach |
| Results | Deeper clean, floor dry sooner | Adequate for light soil |
| Cost | Machine cost, upkeep, battery and charging | Low equipment cost, more labour time |

## When is a machine worth it?

Machines pay back where floors are large, open and hard, such as warehouses, retail floors, school halls and big lobbies. They also suit sites that need the floor dry and safe quickly. In a small office with carpet and a few hard-floor areas, a mop is usually enough.

## How does it affect the quote?

Machine cleaning cuts labour time on large floors, which should be reflected in the price, offset by the contractor's cost of owning and maintaining the machine. Ask how the quote changes if you specify machine cleaning. See [what affects a cleaning quote](/resources/hiring-a-cleaner/pricing-quotes-and-scope/what-affects-a-cleaning-quote/).

## What should you ask a contractor?

Do you bring a machine to this site, what size and type, who maintains and charges it, and how do you handle edges and corners? How is the floor left, and how is it kept safe while wet? Do you hold the right insurance for equipment use on site?

## What about access and storage?

A machine needs somewhere to charge and be stored, and a route through the building. In a shared building you may need permission from the owner. Agree these before the first clean.

## What about safety?

Wet floors are a slip hazard and machines can be a trip or collision hazard in busy areas. Agree the hours and signage.

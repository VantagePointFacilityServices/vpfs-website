Most buyers of commercial cleaning never see the work being done. They see the result, or they see the problems. Understanding the basics of what a good contractor does, which products they use and which machines they bring lets you write a better scope, ask better questions and spot when something is off.

This is written for the person buying or managing cleaning, so you can understand and check what a contractor is doing. It is not a training manual for cleaners.

## Why does it matter to the buyer?

Methods, chemicals and equipment are where cost and quality are decided. A contractor who uses the right method for a floor protects your asset and gets a better result in less time. One who uses the wrong product can damage a surface, create a safety risk or waste your money. The differences are invisible on a quote, which is why it helps to know what to ask.

Cleaning chemicals are the part of a contract that buyers understand least and that carries the most risk if it goes wrong. The words on the label, such as cleaner, sanitiser and disinfectant, mean different things. The safety data sheet tells you what is in a product and how to use it safely. And the products themselves fall into broad families that suit different jobs and can damage the wrong surface.

This is written for the person buying or managing cleaning, so you can understand and check what a contractor is doing. It is not a training manual for cleaners.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Cleaner vs sanitiser vs disinfectant** explains the three terms and what the regulator says about claims.
- **How to read a safety data sheet** shows you what matters on the page.
- **Neutral, alkaline and acid cleaners** covers what each family is for and why mixing is dangerous.

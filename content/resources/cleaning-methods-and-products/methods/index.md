A cleaning method is the way a task is done: which tool, which product, in what order and how often. Two contractors can both say they will "clean the floors" and deliver very different results because their methods differ. Knowing the main methods lets you put the right ones in your scope and recognise them when you inspect.

This is written for the person buying or managing cleaning, so you can understand and check what a contractor is doing. It is not a training manual for cleaners.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Commercial cleaning schedule: daily, weekly and monthly** shows how tasks are spread over time.
- **Commercial carpet care methods** explains vacuuming, spot cleaning and deeper cleaning.
- **Hard-floor maintenance: scrub, burnish and seal** covers the main options for hard floors.
- **Washroom cleaning standard** describes what a proper washroom clean involves.

They sit within the wider [guide to methods, products and equipment](/resources/cleaning-methods-and-products/). If you are specifying a particular building type, see the [guide to cleaning by building type](/resources/by-building-type/). To discuss your site, [book a walkthrough](/contact.html).

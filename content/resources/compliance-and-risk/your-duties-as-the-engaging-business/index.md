Hiring a cleaner does not hand over your safety responsibilities. If you run the workplace, you still have duties to the people who work in it, including contractors, and you must work with the contractor to manage shared risks. These guides explain what that means in practice and how to set up the contractor's arrival properly.

General information for buyers, not legal advice. Check current requirements with the regulator or your own adviser.

## The guides in this cluster

- **WHS duties when you hire a cleaning contractor** explains the idea of shared duties and what consultation, cooperation and coordination look like for a cleaning contract.
- **Contractor induction and site access** shows what to cover before a contractor's staff start work, and how to control access.

Both assume you are choosing a contractor with the checks in [what a compliant contractor holds](/resources/compliance-and-risk/what-a-compliant-contractor-holds/), and they link back to the overall [compliance and risk guide](/resources/compliance-and-risk/).

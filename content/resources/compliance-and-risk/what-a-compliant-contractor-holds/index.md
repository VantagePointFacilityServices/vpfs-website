A compliant contractor can show you, in writing and on request, that its people are checked and properly paid, that it is insured, and that it manages its chemicals safely. These are not extras to negotiate after you have chosen on price. They are the foundation of the contract, and a contractor who cannot supply them is carrying risk that can end up with you.

General information for buyers, not legal advice. Check current requirements with the regulator or your own adviser.

## The guides in this cluster

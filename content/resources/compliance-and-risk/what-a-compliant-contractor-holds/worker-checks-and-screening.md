Ask the contractor which checks it runs on every cleaner before they start and ask them to confirm in writing that those checks have been done for the people on your site. Typical checks cover the right to work in Australia, identity, a police check where appropriate and any screening your type of site requires.

General information for buyers, not legal advice. Check current requirements with the regulator or your own adviser.

## Why do worker checks matter?

Cleaners have access to your building, often unsupervised and out of hours, and sometimes to sensitive areas. Checks reduce the chance of theft, misconduct and unlawful employment, and show you have taken care in choosing who comes into your workplace.

## What checks should you ask about?

- **Right to work:** employers must make sure workers are entitled to work in Australia. Ask how the contractor verifies this for employees and subcontractors.
- **Identity:** how the contractor confirms who each person is.
- **Police check:** whether a national police check is carried out, how recent it is and how it is renewed. Checks need the person's consent and results need to be handled carefully, so ask the contractor to confirm outcomes rather than hand you the documents.
- **Site-specific screening:** schools, childcare, medical and aged-care sites may require particular screening for contractors. Check your own requirements and ask the contractor to confirm their people meet them. See [cleaning schools and early-learning centres](/resources/by-building-type/education-and-childcare/cleaning-schools-and-early-learning-centres/).
- **Training and licences:** induction, manual handling, chemical handling and any licences needed for equipment.

## How do you confirm it?

Ask for a written statement, signed by a director or manager, that the checks have been done for every person assigned to your site, and for the names of those people. Ask what happens when staff change and how new people are checked before their first shift.

## What about relief cleaners?

Absences happen. A reliable contractor has relief staff who are screened and inducted to the same standard. Make this a contract requirement so a stranger never turns up with your keys.

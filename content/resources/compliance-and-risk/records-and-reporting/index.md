If something goes wrong on your site, the first questions are about evidence: what did you check, what did the contractor provide and what was reported when. These guides cover the records worth keeping from the start of a cleaning contract and how incidents and hazards should be reported during it.

General information for buyers, not legal advice. Check current requirements with the regulator or your own adviser.

## The guides in this cluster

- **Documents a contractor should give you** is the checklist of paperwork to collect and keep current.
- **Incident and hazard reporting on site** sets out how events are reported, who reports them and what you must do about serious ones.

They build on [what a compliant contractor holds](/resources/compliance-and-risk/what-a-compliant-contractor-holds/) and [your duties as the engaging business](/resources/compliance-and-risk/your-duties-as-the-engaging-business/), and tie back to the overall [compliance and risk guide](/resources/compliance-and-risk/).

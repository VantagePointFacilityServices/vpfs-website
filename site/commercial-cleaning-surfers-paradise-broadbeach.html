Surfers Paradise and Broadbeach

Cleaning for the high-rise coastal strip

Two G:link stations serve Broadbeach: Broadbeach South, out the front of Pacific Fair Shopping Centre, and Broadbeach North, in front of the Gold Coast Convention and Exhibition Centre on the Gold Coast Highway. Light rail services run about every 15 minutes, seven days a week, connecting Broadbeach with Surfers Paradise.

Most of the commercial buildings along this strip are apartment towers, with offices, hospitality and retail on the lower floors, so a clean here is often a strata lobby and bin-room job rather than a tenancy fit-out.

Building mix

Strata towers, mixed-use podiums and event-adjacent offices

The coastal strip is dense and vertical. Most work here is shared property rather than a single tenancy.

Beachfront strata towers

Lobbies, lifts, bin rooms, car parks and amenity areas, with reporting that a committee can read.

Mixed-use podium buildings

Offices above retail or hospitality, where commercial and residential areas share lifts and loading docks.

Offices near the convention centre

Tenancies close to the Broadbeach North station and event precinct, cleaned around the building’s event days.

Access and drive time

Getting onto the strip from Reedy Creek

Surfers Paradise and Broadbeach are usually a drive of about 20 to 30 minutes from our Reedy Creek base, depending on traffic and event days.

Many towers have shared loading docks, basement car parks with limited height and building-manager lift bookings. Streets are busy with pedestrians and rideshare, so we plan arrival with the manager first and, for strata work, agree where the crew stages equipment.

Inspected by someone who didn’t do the clean

A cleaner marking their own work isn’t a check. After the clean, a supervisor who wasn’t on the roster inspects the site and scores it against your written scope. You receive a monthly cleaning report covering the agreed scope.

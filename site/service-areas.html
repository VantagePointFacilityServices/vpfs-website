Why We Stay Selective

Coverage isn’t the goal — consistency is

The same team

Taking on fewer clients means we can keep the same team on your site, rather than a rotating pool spread across the coast.

A supervisor who can actually attend

Selective coverage means our supervisors can reach your site quickly, so quality checks happen in person.

An account manager, not a call centre

A manageable client base is what makes it possible for one person to genuinely know your business.

Room to grow, without diluting the model

We’re building the systems to scale — but only as far as they can carry the same standard, not further.

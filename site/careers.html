Requirements

What we ask of you

This is for experienced commercial cleaning technicians. You don’t need to be perfect, but you do need these.

  • The right to work in Australia
  • A police check (we ask for it after your interview, not before)
  • At least 1 year of commercial cleaning experience
  • Your own reliable transport
  • A Blue Card, for school or childcare sites only

Not sure whether something applies to you? Email careers@vantagepointfacilityservices.com.au.

The work

What the work looks like

You should know what you are applying for before you apply, so here it is.

The sites

We clean offices, medical and dental centres, strata buildings and education sites, along with some other commercial sites. Each one has a written scope, so you know which areas and tasks are yours before you walk in.

The hours

Most of our work happens outside business hours: early mornings, evenings and nights. Our first intake is likely to be weekend and public-holiday casual shifts. We’d rather say that now than have you find out at the interview.

A typical shift

  1. Sign in on Connecteam when you arrive.
  2. Work through the site’s written scope.
  3. Complete the checklist, with photos.
  4. Sign off and head home.

The physical side

Cleaning is physical work. You’ll be on your feet, lifting and emptying bins, bending, kneeling, vacuuming and mopping. If you have questions about any of it, ask us.

What we provide. Your uniform and PPE are issued at induction. We supply the equipment and chemicals, so there is nothing to buy and no supply runs.

Pay

Good work, paid above the award

Employees are paid under the Cleaning Services Award 2020, and we pay good cleaning technicians above it. The award starts at $27.08 an hour (Level 1), or $33.85 an hour for casuals, which includes the casual loading. Higher classifications and more experience mean more. You are paid weekly, every week.

Award rates from 1 July 2026. Check the official pay guide.

What comes with the job

Paid weekly

Your pay lands every week, so you always know where you stand.

Uniform and PPE issued at induction

You are kitted out before your first site, and you don’t pay for it.

Equipment and chemicals supplied

Everything you need for the scope is provided. There is nothing to buy and no supply runs.

The same sites, a small team

Wherever possible, we keep you on the same sites with the same small team, so you learn each site properly.

Quarterly quality bonuses

Good work should show up in your pay. Quality bonuses are paid each quarter and are based on site inspection results and client feedback. Attendance alone doesn’t earn one, so the work itself is what counts.

Career ladder

Build a career in cleaning, not just a shift.

Here is the path we’re building. Each step has a clear bar to reach, so you know where you stand and what to work towards. We’re a small company, so you’ll be seen and not lost in a large one.

  1. Cleaning Technician. You’ve passed probation and finished your site training.
  2. Senior Cleaning Technician. 6+ months with us, two consecutive quarters at the standard, one extra training module, and you help new starters find their feet.
  3. Site Lead / Trainer. 12+ months with us, you run site inductions, and you spot-check the work of your peers.
  4. Cleaning Supervisor.
  5. Operations Manager.

We list the bar for the first three steps today. Supervisor and Operations Manager roles open as the company grows.

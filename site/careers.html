How We Work With You

Two ways to join us, one standard either way

Nothing here is a perk. It is what you are entitled to, written down so you can hold us to it.

Subcontractor cleaners — our current model

We work with vetted, fully insured subcontractor cleaning businesses: your own ABN, your own equipment and insurance, a signed subcontractor agreement setting out rates and terms up front.

Direct employment — where we’re heading

As our recurring client base grows to support it, we transition to directly employed roles under the Cleaning Services Award 2020 (MA000022), with the Fair Work Information Statement provided at or before your start date.

Structured onboarding, either way

Every new team member is shown our documented walkthrough and inspection standard before working a site solo — not sent out to work it out alone.

Somewhere to raise a concern

Your point of contact is the first port of call for any issue on site — and it’s always a real person, not a ticketing system.

What We’re Looking For

Vetted, reliable, and easy to work with

Active ABN & insurance
Police check / clearances
Own equipment, reliably maintained
Punctual and consistent availability

Our approach

Four commitments behind every clean.

Independent quality audits

Your crew works to the same checklist every visit, with before-and-after photos. Each month a supervisor who didn’t do the clean inspects your site against the written scope, and the score goes into your monthly report card.

Colour-coded cleaning system

Each area has its own colour-coded microfibre, so the cloth used in your washrooms never touches your kitchen. Every product is measured to the correct dilution. If anyone on your team is sensitive to smells, tell us and we’ll review the products together. Safety data sheets are available on request.

High-touch hygiene focus

Microfibre picks up dirt and germs more effectively than cotton cloths and mops. We give extra attention to the surfaces everyone touches, such as door handles, taps, light switches and lift buttons, to help reduce germs, dust and allergens across your building.

Single point of contact

One person who knows your site handles everything, so you never have to chase. Report an issue once: urgent problems are fixed straight away, and anything minor is resolved by the next visit.

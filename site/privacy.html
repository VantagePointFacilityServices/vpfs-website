1. About this policy

This policy explains how Vantage Point Facility Services Pty Ltd (ACN 700 018 775, ABN 89 700 018 775) handles personal information. We trade as Vantage Point Facility Services (commercial cleaning) and Vantage Point Cleaning (residential cleaning). "We", "us" and "our" mean the company under either brand.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. Whose information we collect

  • Clients and prospective clients: people who enquire, request a quote, book or receive our services, including property and facility managers, business contacts and householders.
  • People who call us: including calls answered by our AI receptionist.
  • Job applicants: people who apply to work with us as employees or subcontractors.
  • Suppliers and subcontractors: their business contacts.
  • People referred to us: when an existing client refers someone.
  • Website visitors.

This policy doesn't cover our employees' employee records, which are handled under our employment policies.

4. How we collect it

Mostly directly from you: through our websites and forms, by phone (including our AI receptionist), SMS, email, and when our team visits your site. Sometimes from others, for example a property manager who books on behalf of an owner, a client who refers you, or referees you nominate when you apply for a job.

5. Why we collect and use it

  • To quote, book, deliver, check and invoice our services.
  • To answer calls and enquiries, including qualifying enquiries and booking walkthroughs through our AI receptionist and website forms.
  • To resolve complaints, guarantee claims and disputes, using our site records and photos.
  • To assess job applications and meet screening requirements for client sites.
  • To meet legal obligations, for example tax, insurance and work health and safety.
  • To send marketing, only if you've agreed. You can opt out at any time (section 9).

Automated processing. Our AI receptionist answers calls, transcribes them and records your answers. Our website and phone enquiry process uses an automated score, based on details such as site size, cleaning frequency and budget, to decide which booking path an enquiry is offered. A person reviews enquiries flagged by the score, and you can always ask to speak to someone.

7. Overseas disclosure

Some of our providers store information outside Australia, in the countries listed in section 6: mainly the USA, the United Kingdom, Germany and the Netherlands, and other countries where our providers operate. We choose providers with recognised security and privacy practices, and take reasonable steps to make sure they handle your information in line with the APPs.

8. Security and retention

We store information in secure, access-controlled systems with multi-factor authentication. We keep it only as long as we need it, or as long as the law requires:

  • tax and financial records: 5 years;
  • employment records: 7 years;
  • site photos: 12 months from the visit, longer only while a dispute or claim is open.

9. Marketing

We only send marketing emails and SMS if you've agreed, separately from agreeing to our terms. Every message lets you unsubscribe, and you can also contact us to opt out.

10. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at blake@vantagepointfacilityservices.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first at blake@vantagepointfacilityservices.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on our websites, with the date it was last updated.

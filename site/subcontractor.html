What you get

What subcontracting with us looks like

The business side is simple. We win and manage the clients; you deliver the scope.

Regular recurring sites

The same recurring sites, so you can plan your business around them. As you show you can look after a site well, there is more on offer.

A site induction

We walk you through each site and its written scope before you start. Induction also covers emergency response, the SDS register, the key register, Connecteam and confidentiality.

Job details in Connecteam

Site details and scope in one place, on your phone. You always know what the client expects at each site.

No chasing clients for payment

We deal with the client, so you don’t have to. You run your business, and we manage the client relationship.

  • No franchise fee and no joining fee.
  • You can send your own workers, once they’re police-checked and inducted before their first site.
  • This is a subcontract, not employment.
  • We win and manage the clients; you deliver the scope.

What the work is

The sites are commercial: offices, medical and dental centres, strata buildings and education sites. Every site has a written scope that sets out the areas and tasks the client expects, and the job details sit in Connecteam so you can check them on your phone. Before your first site we run an induction. Your first job with us is a paid trial, and each month we send a report card on how the work is going. You run the business and deliver the scope in your own way, with your own equipment and insurance.

Growth

Room to grow your business with us

As you show you can look after a site well, we can offer you more sites, and larger contracts as they come up. Your own workers can come with you once they’re police-checked and inducted. When we begin hiring employees, contractors who have worked with us will get the first offer.

Requirements

What we ask of you

You run your own business, so we ask for the following.

  • An ABN
  • Public liability insurance
  • Your own equipment
  • Your own reliable transport
  • The right to work in Australia
  • A police check (we collect it after your interview)
  • At least 1 year of commercial cleaning experience
  • A Blue Card, for school or childcare sites only

Not sure whether something applies to you? Email careers@vantagepointfacilityservices.com.au.

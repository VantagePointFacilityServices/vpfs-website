Who this is for

School business managers and facilities coordinators who need the clean done before the students arrive

If you run the facilities side of a school on the Gold Coast, the clean has to be finished and out of the way before the first bell, and the big jobs have to fit in the gaps between terms. We schedule the routine clean outside school hours and plan holiday deep cleans around your term calendar.

This page is for primary and secondary schools, including independent and boarding campuses, that want one provider on a written scope. If you are weighing school cleaning companies, ask each one for the scope in writing and for how the work is checked.

What’s in the written scope

A school scope covering classrooms, toilets, staff rooms and offices

This is the standard school clean our crew works to. Your written scope sets out which of these apply to each building, and how often.

  • Classrooms: bins emptied, desks and surfaces wiped, floors vacuumed or mopped
  • Student and staff toilets cleaned fixture by fixture with the correct colour-coded cloth, and consumables restocked
  • Staff rooms and kitchenettes: benches, sinks, appliance exteriors, tables
  • Administration offices and front reception
  • Corridors, stairwells and communal areas
  • Touchpoints wiped on every visit: door handles, light switches, handrails, shared equipment
  • Hard floors cleaned, with floors done last
  • Libraries, hallways and multipurpose halls on the schedule you set
  • Holiday deep-clean scheduling planned around term dates
  • After-hours access and alarm procedures agreed with the school
How quality is checked

Inspected by someone who didn’t do the clean

A cleaner marking their own work isn’t a check. After the clean, a supervisor who wasn’t on the roster inspects the site and scores it against your written scope.

You receive a monthly cleaning report covering the agreed scope, so you see the result without having to ask.

Method

How the crew works through a campus

Outside school hours

Routine cleaning is scheduled after the school day, so cleaners are not working in rooms while lessons are running.

Holiday deep cleans

Larger jobs are booked into term breaks and agreed ahead of time, so rooms are back in use when the term starts.

Colour-coded microfibre

Red for toilets and urinals, yellow for restroom surfaces, blue for general surfaces and glass, green for kitchens and staff rooms. A cloth never crosses categories.

Commercial Cleaning · Gold Coast · About Us

A Boutique Commercial Cleaning Company for the Gold Coast

Vantage Point Facility Services is family owned and founder-led, built on the Gold Coast by someone who got tired of hoping a contractor's promises would hold. Here's who's actually behind the business you'd be trusting with your building — for the standard itself, see Why us.

Founder

Built by someone who used to sign the cheques

Blake Warton founded Vantage Point Facility Services on the Gold Coast to bring a documented, accountable standard to commercial cleaning — the same standard the best strata committees and facility managers wish every contractor already met.

As CEO, Blake does not perform the cleaning — the role is deliberately kept on strategy, client relationships and quality assurance, so the business runs on documented systems rather than one person's memory. That means a scope that survives staff changes, an audit process nobody can talk their way around, and a founder who still personally reads every monthly report before it reaches your inbox.

What we stand for

Value over price

We compete on outcomes and accountability, not the lowest quote. A cheaper contractor that misses corners costs more in the end.

Data over promises

Service quality is demonstrated through inspection records and monthly reporting, not claimed on a phone call.

Systems compound

Every repeatable task becomes a written procedure, so quality does not depend on which cleaner is rostered that week.

Compliant by default

Fair Work, WHS and privacy obligations are designed in from the start, not retrofitted after a complaint.

Our people

Fewer clients, not less care

Vantage Point deliberately serves a smaller number of higher-value sites rather than competing on volume — the same boutique reasoning behind firms in law, medicine and finance. Teams are matched to a building instead of being spread across a rotation, starting on vetted contractors and moving to a directly employed workforce as recurring work grows.

  • Boutique client count, not high-volume contracts
  • Teams matched to your building, not rotated across a roster
  • Vetted contractors today, transitioning to direct employment as we grow

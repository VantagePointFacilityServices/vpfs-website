A Gold Coast Family Business, Led by Its Founder

Vantage Point Facility Services is family owned and founder-led. Blake grew up on the Gold Coast and looks after commercial cleaning for businesses across South East Queensland and the Northern Rivers. For the standard itself, see Why us.

Founder

Meet Blake Warton

Blake is a Gold Coast local. He grew up in Currumbin and Palm Beach, went to Palm Beach High, and spent his childhood summers at Currumbin nippers. He has surfed all over the Gold Coast, including with the local surf clubs.

He loves to travel and has been to plenty of countries. He has seen how it's done around the world, and chose to build his business at home.

Blake is proud of his Aboriginal, Chinese and English/Irish heritage. He's a single dad to two boys, aged 7 and 4, and nothing beats time with them.

By day he's a software engineer at amaysim, part of Optus. That's why Vantage Point runs on written systems, checklists and data rather than memory.

Blake doesn't do the cleaning himself. His job is making sure it's done right, so busy business owners can get their time back and have real peace of mind.

What we stand for

We want partnerships and friendships where clients, cleaners and partners all do well, support their families and enjoy the South East Queensland lifestyle.

Value over price

We compete on outcomes and accountability, not the lowest quote. A cheaper contractor that misses corners costs more in the end.

Data over promises

Service quality is demonstrated through inspection records and monthly reporting, not claimed on a phone call.

Systems compound

Every repeatable task becomes a written procedure, so quality does not depend on which cleaner is rostered that week.

Compliant by default

Fair Work, WHS and privacy obligations are designed in from the start, not retrofitted after a complaint.

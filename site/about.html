How we work

Why we run on systems, not memory

By day, Blake is a software engineer at amaysim, part of Optus. Good software teams don't rely on memory. They write things down, check their work and keep a record. Blake brought that habit to cleaning.

He doesn't do the cleaning himself. His job is making sure it's done right, every time. So every building gets a room-by-room cleaning plan, every procedure is written down, and every month you get a report card. The standard doesn't depend on who's rostered that week, or on anyone remembering.

Our standard

What “done properly” means to us

For Blake, being professional isn't a polo shirt and a logo. It's turning up when we said we would, cleaning what's on the plan, showing you the photos, and owning it when something's missed.

That's why he started with the boring parts most cleaning companies skip. Before any cleaning started, he wrote down how every routine job is done: how a site is opened and locked up, how chemicals are measured, which colour cloth goes where, what a cleaning technician wears and how they behave on your site. New cleaning technicians learn from those procedures, not from whoever trained them last.

It also means being honest about what we are. We're a young, local business, and we'd rather show you how we work than make big claims. If something isn't up to standard, we fix the process so it doesn't happen again.

What we stand for

We want partnerships and friendships where clients, cleaning technicians and partners all do well, support their families and enjoy the South East Queensland lifestyle.

Value over price

We won't be the cheapest quote, and we'll tell you that up front. A cleaner who cuts corners usually costs more in the end.

Data over promises

Anyone can promise a great clean on the phone. We'd rather show you: a room-by-room cleaning plan before we start, and a report card with photos every month.

Systems compound

Every repeatable job gets written down. When something works, it becomes the standard. When something slips, we fix the process, not just the problem.

Compliant by default

Fair pay, safe work and privacy are built in from day one, not bolted on after a complaint.

Local families

Good work for local families

Good cleaning comes down to good people who feel looked after. Our cleaners are paid at or above award-equivalent rates and get steady, local work close to home. We match a team to your building and aim to keep the same team on your site, so they learn its quirks and you get to know their faces. We're building the kind of team people want to stay in: fair pay, clear standards, respect for the work, and people who back each other. Right now that team is vetted local contractors, and we'll move to direct employment as our regular work grows.
